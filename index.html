Know Your Neighborhood

What We See in Carrollwood Yards

Established neighborhoods come with established challenges. We plan for them before we quote.

Aging Wood Fences

Many homes still have an original or decades-old wood fence. We remove it, haul it away, and set new posts rather than building on old ones.

Big Oaks & Roots

Mature trees are part of Carrollwood's charm. We route the fence line and set posts to avoid damaging major roots wherever we can.

Lakefront Lots

Around Lake Carroll and the area's other lakes, open aluminum keeps the view while still enclosing the yard.

Backyard Pools

Many homes here have a pool. We build pool barriers with self-closing, self-latching gates to Florida code.

Made for established neighborhoods

Fences that fit mature trees, older lot lines, and Carrollwood's deed restrictions.
