Fence Gates

Gates for Carrollwood yards, from side-yard walk gates to drive gates.

Request a Free Estimate Call 813-669-4555

On Carrollwood's established lots, side yards are often narrow and the old gates are usually the first thing to fail. We build gates that fit tight spaces and stay square.

What You Get

✓Narrow side-yard gates
✓Double drive gates
✓Gate-only replacements
✓Frames that prevent sagging
✓Durable hinges and latches

Sizing for your yard

Narrow side yards often fit only a 3' or 4' walk gate. If you need a mower through, aim for 5'. For a boat or trailer, a 10' to 12' double gate is standard.

Replacing the gate, not the whole fence

If the fence is sound but the gate drags or won't latch, a new gate on a properly set post is often all you need. We can match your existing fence or build a new gate in a material that holds up better than the original.

Swing or slide

Swing gates need clear, level ground. Where roots, beds or slopes get in the way, a cantilever slide gate runs on its own track, provided there's room alongside.

Making it last

Old gates fail at the hinge post. We set gate posts deeper with more concrete, frame vinyl and wood gates with metal and use hardware that resists humidity.
